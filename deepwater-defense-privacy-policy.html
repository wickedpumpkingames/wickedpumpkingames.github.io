Deepwater Defense Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Who we are

Deepwater Defense is made by Wicked Pumpkin Games. We don't collect personal information ourselves: the game keeps your progress on your phone, and only Google's ad and payment services receive data, as described below.

This policy covers the Deepwater Defense app for Android and takes effect on November 4, 2026.

Information stored on your device

Your saved games, settings and unlocked items are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. There is no account to create, we run no servers, and your saves are never sent to us.

The game plays offline. It goes online only to load ads and to make or restore Google Play purchases. If you have allowed it in Android, Google Play may also collect crash and performance reports under Google's own policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

From level 5 on, the game shows a full-screen ad before battles and dives, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app collects:

  • Your device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Which ads were shown to you and whether you tapped them

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any personalized ads. You can refuse personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and manage ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Ad Free, sold in the game's Treasure Chest, turns ads off for good.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google handles your payment details, and we never see them.

The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought (the product and a purchase token), so it can give you the item. That stays on your phone. Restore Purchases asks Google Play which items your account owns.

Sharing

The game can make a picture of your ship and progress to share. It's created on your phone and goes only where you send it, through Android's share menu, when you tap Share. Nothing is shared automatically.

Children

Deepwater Defense is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given information through the game's ad or payment services, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and contact

  • Deleting your data: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Deepwater Defense → Storage → Clear storage. That erases your saves. Data held by Google for ads and purchases is managed in your Google Account.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal data about you. We hold none ourselves, but we're happy to help with any request.
  • Security: data sent to Google's services travels encrypted.
  • Changes: if this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
