Deepwater Defense Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Who we are

Deepwater Defense is made by Wicked Pumpkin Games. We don't collect personal information ourselves: the game keeps your progress on your phone (and, if you use Google Play Games, backs it up to your Google account), and only Google's ad, payment and Play Games services receive data, as described below.

This policy covers the Deepwater Defense app for Android and takes effect on November 17, 2026.

Information stored on your device

Your saved games, settings and unlocked items are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. There is no account to create with us, we run no servers, and your saves are never sent to us.

The game plays offline. It goes online only to load ads, to make or restore Google Play purchases, and to back up your saves to Google Play Games. If you have allowed it in Android, Google Play may also collect crash and performance reports under Google's own policies.

Cloud saves (Google Play Games)

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game backs up your saved games and unlocked items to Saved Games in your Google account. Your progress then comes back on a new phone or after you reinstall. Your settings stay on the phone.

The game also reads your Play Games name, only to show it in Settings. Wicked Pumpkin Games never receives or stores any of this: Google keeps it under the Google Privacy Policy, and Play Games may collect diagnostics about its own service.

To stop cloud saves, sign out of Google Play Games on your phone. You can delete your Play Games data, including this game's cloud saves, from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

From level 5 on, the game shows a full-screen ad before battles and dives, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app collects:

  • Your device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Which ads were shown to you and whether you tapped them

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any personalized ads. You can refuse personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and manage ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Ad Free, sold in the game's Treasure Chest, turns ads off for good.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google handles your payment details, and we never see them.

The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought (the product and a purchase token), so it can give you the item. That stays on your phone. Restore Purchases asks Google Play which items your account owns.

Sharing

The game can make a picture of your ship and progress to share. It's created on your phone and goes only where you send it, through Android's share menu, when you tap Share. Nothing is shared automatically.

Children

Deepwater Defense is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given information through the game's ad, payment or Play Games services, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and contact

  • Deleting your data: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Deepwater Defense → Storage → Clear storage. That erases the saves on your phone. Your cloud saves in Google Play Games, and data Google holds for ads and purchases, are managed in your Google Account.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal data about you. We hold none ourselves, but we're happy to help with any request.
  • Security: data sent to Google's services travels encrypted.
  • Changes: if this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Questions? Contact Wicked Pumpkin Games at WickedPumpkinLLC@gmail.com.
