Deepwater Defense

Ten seas. Ten bosses. One very big Kraken.

Somewhere past the edge of the last chart lies the Island of Riches. Between you and it are ten seas of hungry sea monsters and a Kraken the size of a small kingdom. Arm your galleon, recruit legendary captains and race Captain Silas Greedmore, the richest pirate on the seven seas (just ask him), to the gold.

Every sea fights differently. Monsters fly over your nets, dive under your cannonballs, hide in the dark, wear armor, freeze your guns or laugh off fire. Every sea ends with a boss, from King Pincher, a crab nearly as big as your ship, to the Kraken itself.

All hands on deck

Everything you can bring aboard on the way to the gold.

30 weapons

Cannons, harpoons, nets, mortars, lightning coils, flame projectors, torpedoes and more. Set the right ones side by side for 30 formations, or combine three of a kind into one of 9 giant weapons.

13 captains

Each brings a weapon you fire yourself: Blackbeard’s blunderbuss, Hookhand Hattie’s grapnel, Morwenna the Sea Witch’s whirlpools and more. Most bring a support ship, too.

12 pets

Rescue sea creatures from Chomper the shark to Old Barnacle the whale and send them into the fight. When it all goes wrong, toss a bucket of chum overboard.

10 treasure dives

Pull on the brass helmet and go over the side. Every sea has its own dive site, laid out differently every time. Watch your air and mind the dark.

Also aboard: 100 levels with a story told in comic scenes, a Daily Voyage every day, the endless Maelstrom, Medium and Hard difficulties and 58 achievements. Battery saver, larger text and a colorblind mode are built in.

Deepwater Defense is free to play, with optional in-app purchases and ads, and plays offline. Prefer no ads? Ad Free is available in the game’s Treasure Chest.
