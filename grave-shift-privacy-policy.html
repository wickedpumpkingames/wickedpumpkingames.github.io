Grave Shift Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Who we are

Grave Shift is a game for Android made by Wicked Pumpkin Games ("we", "us"). We don't run servers, and we don't collect personal information ourselves. Your game is kept on your phone and, if you use cloud saves, in your own Google Play Games account. Google's ad, payment, cloud-save and crash-reporting services receive some data, as described below.

This policy covers the Grave Shift app for Android.

Information stored on your device

Your saved games, settings and unlocked items (including Ad Free and Keeper looks) are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. So is a record of your purchases (which item, its order number and the date), which the game uses to make sure every purchase is delivered once. Names you give your in-game pets are part of your save. There is no account to create with us, and nothing you store in the game is sent to us.

The game plays offline. It goes online only to show ads, to make or restore purchases, to back up your saves to Google Play Games, to send crash reports, and to show Google's rating pop-up.

Cloud saves (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game backs up your save slots and your account record (Ad Free, Keeper looks, purchase records and progress toward earned looks) to Saved Games in your Google Play Games account, so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game or change phones. This data is stored by Google under your Google account. We cannot see it. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can delete it from your Google Play Games settings or your Google Account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

From the third day of a new game, a full-screen ad is shown after you open the gates in the morning and after you start the night. The Treasure Chest also offers optional Free Treasure ads: short ads you choose to watch in return for an in-game prize. The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app collects:

  • Your device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Which ads were shown to you, and whether you tapped or finished watching them

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any personalized ads, and you can refuse personalized ads there.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some phones), and manage ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Ad Free, sold in the game's Treasure Chest, turns off the morning and night ads for good. Free Treasure ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are made through Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google handles your payment details, and we never see them.

The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought (the item, an order number and a purchase token), so it can give you the item. That record stays on your phone and in your cloud save. Restore Purchases asks Google Play which items your account owns.

Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or runs into an error, a report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • The error and where in the game's code it happened
  • Your phone model, Android version and app version
  • A random installation ID created by Crashlytics, used to count how many players a problem affects
  • Where you were in the game when it happened: the game day, day or night, and which screen was open

Reports don't include your name, your email address or the contents of your saves. Crashlytics keeps crash reports and their identifiers for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Ratings

At a few moments in the game, Google Play may show its own "rate this app" pop-up. It is handled entirely by Google Play. We only see a rating or review if you choose to post one, the same as any public review on Google Play.

Children

Grave Shift is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given information through the game's ad, payment, cloud-save or crash-reporting services, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and contact

  • Deleting your data: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Grave Shift → Storage → Clear storage. That erases the saves on your phone. Cloud saves are deleted from your Google Play Games settings. Data held by Google for ads, purchases and Play Games is managed in your Google Account.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal data about you. We hold none ourselves, but we're happy to help with any request.
  • No selling: we don't sell your personal information. Data goes only to the Google services described above, which provide the game's ads, purchases, cloud saves and crash reports.
  • Security: data sent to Google's services travels encrypted.
  • Changes: if this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
